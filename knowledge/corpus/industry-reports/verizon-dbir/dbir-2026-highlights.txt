Verizon 2026 Data Breach Investigations Report — Highlights

Ransomware remains the leading cause of breaches, featuring in roughly a
quarter of all incidents. Extortion continues to rise as attackers pivot from
encryption to data theft and the threat of public release.

Human error remains a material cause. Misconfiguration and misdelivery are
persistent contributors, and the phishing vector continues to grow.

Supply chain compromise features in a meaningful share of breaches, with
third-party software and managed service providers serving as initial access
points.

Business email compromise remains a high-frequency, lower-severity threat,
with wire-transfer fraud the most common outcome.

For a large firm, the likelihood of disclosing at least one breach within a
twelve-month period is substantial. The report underscores that control
maturity — patching cadence, multi-factor authentication coverage, and
incident response readiness — materially reduces both frequency and impact.
