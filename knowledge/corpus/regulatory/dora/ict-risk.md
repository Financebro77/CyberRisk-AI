# DORA ICT Risk Management

Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 establishes a comprehensive framework on digital
operational resilience for the financial sector.

## Scope

DORA applies to financial entities including credit institutions, payment
institutions, investment firms, and insurance undertakings. Third-party ICT
service providers that support critical functions are also in scope.

## ICT Risk Management

Financial entities must establish a sound, comprehensive and well-documented
ICT risk management framework. The framework covers identification,
protection and prevention, detection, response and recovery, and learning
and evolving.

## Third-Party Risk

Entities must implement a strategy on ICT third-party risk, maintain a
register of information on contractual arrangements, and perform due
diligence before entering arrangements that support critical or important
functions.

## Incident Reporting

Major ICT-related incidents must be reported to the competent authority.
Entities must establish an incident reporting process and classify incidents
by severity, materiality and impact.

## Resilience Testing

Entities must carry out digital operational resilience testing appropriate to
their size, business model and risk. Large entities are required to conduct
threat-led penetration testing at least every three years.
